# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Homebound Remit serves diaspora communities — people sending money home to family across borders. We hold ourselves to the same standard of respect we want those families to receive.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

**Expected:**
- Respectful, constructive communication
- Welcoming feedback and different viewpoints
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people this project serves

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/Homebound-Remit/homebound-remit/security/advisories/new) or contacting the maintainers directly.

Maintainers will review and respond within 72 hours. Violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.
